What is CKDu?

Chronic kidney disease of unknown etiology (CKDu) is the gradual loss of kidney function over periods of months or years with symptoms appearing in the late stages of the disease. Emergence of CKDu is a global health concern affecting agricultural communities worldwide. It has become increasingly prevalent in developing countries, including Sri Lanka, India, and parts of Latin America. Although the prevalence of CKDu has progressively become more widespread, the underlying molecular mechanisms of the disease remain largely unknown.

BIOM supports CKDu research by organizing datasets, variables, and biomarkers for comparative analysis.

What is BIOM?

BIOM is a database that lets you explore CKDu‑related biomarkers, studies, and structured variables. Many biomarkers are curated from the literature and novel findings identified by our research collaborators. Researchers can also contribute new CKDu‑related biomarkers through our submission workflows.
