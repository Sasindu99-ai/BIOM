Understanding CKDu (Chronic Kidney Disease of Unknown Etiology)

{{ cmsSettings.ckdu_description|default:"Chronic kidney disease of unknown etiology (CKDu) is the gradual loss of kidney function over periods of months or years with symptoms appearing in the late stages of the disease. Emergence of CKDu is a global health concern affecting agricultural communities worldwide. It has become increasingly prevalent in developing countries, including Sri Lanka, India, and parts of Latin America. Although the prevalence of CKDu has progressively become more widespread, the underlying molecular mechanisms of the disease remain largely unknown." }}

Conventional clinical staging relies heavily on serum creatinine and estimated GFR, which often detect renal impairment only after irreversible tubular injury has taken place. BIOM integrates multi-omics (genomics, transcriptomics, proteomics, metabolomics) with geographic and demographic data to uncover predictive biomarkers.
