Our Mission

To provide researchers, clinical nephrologists, and epidemiologists around the world with open, highly curated, and standardized multi-omics data for CKDu. By bridging clinical phenotype data with molecular signatures, we aim to accelerate early biomarker discovery and non-invasive diagnosis.

BIOM facilitates cross-study meta-analyses, eliminates data silos, and promotes reproducible computational biology workflows through direct Python SDK integration.

The CKDu Challenge

Chronic Kidney Disease of Unknown Etiology (CKDu) disproportionately impacts agricultural workers in low- and middle-income tropical countries. Unlike traditional CKD driven by hypertension or diabetes, CKDu manifests primarily as tubulointerstitial damage without typical clinical hallmarks until end stages.

A major barrier to mechanistic breakthroughs has been fragmented datasets, heterogeneous measurement assays, and conflicting phenotypic definitions. BIOM resolves these disparities through rigorous ontology harmonization.

Architecture

The BIOM Data Pipeline

From multi-center field sample collection to reproducible computational workflows.

1
Clinical Curation

Prospective cohorts and community screenings with documented eGFR stages, geographic GPS coordinates, and demographics.

2
Multi-Omics Assays

Urine and serum proteomics, targeted metabolomics, and genomic panels capturing oxidative stress and tubular cell degradation.

3
Harmonization

Automated deduplication, date conflict reconciliation, unit normalization, and structured study variable schemas.

4
Open Access

Direct discovery via interactive web browsers, REST APIs with OpenAPI specifications, and the biom-kit Python distribution.

Data Governance

Commitment to FAIR Data Principles

All records adhere to ethical protocols approved by relevant institutional review boards (IRBs). Patient records are strictly de-identified with cryptographic identifiers, ensuring complete privacy compliance.
